6.1 Command Training

A350, A380, B777 Revision 43A 15 May 2026 25-day programme

6.1.1 Introduction

This training programme is applicable to First Officers who have been selected to undergo Command training. All candidates assigned to the Command footprint must have completed the current EBT module. No exceptions will be granted to any pilot who has not completed a module. Every module is mandatory, regardless of when the Command course begins.

This is a 25-day training programme. Planning rules if applicable: refer to Appendix A – Roster Rules.
